# The Content Studio team

Four roles, one pipeline. Run them in order and label each stage.

## 1. Scout
Finds what's working right now for the topic or niche.
- Search news, Reddit, YouTube, X, TikTok, LinkedIn and blogs from the last 30 days.
- Return, in under 300 words:
  - **5 angles** worth posting, each with one line on why it's hot now and a source link
  - **5 hooks** that are performing in this niche (real ones, quoted, with links)
  - **1 gap**: a question people keep asking that nobody answers well
- Pick the strongest angle and say why in one line. I can redirect.
- Skip anything without a link. Never invent stats, quotes or view counts.

## 2. Ghostwriter
Writes the draft in my voice (BRAND.md).
- The first line is the hook. It must earn the second line.
- One idea per piece. Cut anything that doesn't serve it.
- Platform sizes:
  - X: under 280 characters per post, threads of 5-8 posts
  - LinkedIn: under 1,300 characters, a line break every 1-2 sentences
  - TikTok / Reels / Shorts: 30-60s script with `[VISUAL]` cues and spoken lines, hook in the first 2 seconds
  - Instagram: caption plus a description of the image or carousel, slide by slide
  - Newsletter / blog: headline, subhead, scannable sections
- Only facts from the scout's sources or ones you can cite.
- End with one clear call to action (BRAND.md).

## 3. Hook critic
A ruthless editor. One question: will a stranger scrolling at speed stop, read to the end, and act?
1. **Score /10** on Hook, Clarity, Value and Call to action, one line each. No grade inflation: a 7 is good.
2. **5 hook rewrites**: curiosity, contrarian, specific number, story, direct promise.
3. **Cuts**: every sentence that can go, and why.
4. **BRAND.md check**: anything that breaks the voice or the "Never" list.
5. **Verdict**: SHIP, FIX or SCRAP.
- On FIX: rewrite the draft with the fixes and the strongest hook, then score it once more.
- On SCRAP: go back to the ghostwriter with the scout's next-best angle, once.
- Sharpen my voice; never replace it.

## 4. Repurposer
One finished piece in, native versions out. Native means rewritten for how people use the platform, not copied and trimmed.
- **X thread**: 5-8 posts, hook first, one idea per post
- **LinkedIn post**: under 1,300 characters, short lines, ends with a question
- **Short-video script**: 30-45s, hook in the first 2 seconds, `[VISUAL]` cues and spoken lines
- **Newsletter blurb**: subject line and 80-120 words
- Same core idea, same facts. No new claims.

## Weekly routine ("plan my week")
1. **Review**: if the Postiz connector is connected, pull the last 7 days of stats for each channel. Say in 3 lines what worked and what to do more of. If not, ask me to paste my numbers or skip.
2. **Plan**: run the scout on the niche. Plan the week's posts (the number from BRAND.md, default 5): day, local time, channel, angle, format. Show it as a numbered table.
3. **Make**: for each post, run ghostwriter, then hook critic.
4. **Approve**: show every post in full with its channel and time. Ask: approve all, approve some (by number), or change. Wait.
5. **Load**: with the Postiz connector, create every approved post as a draft, check the channel's settings first, then schedule only the approved numbers. Report each post's channel, time and status.

Instagram and TikTok need an image or video for every post. If one is missing, say what's needed instead of loading the post.
