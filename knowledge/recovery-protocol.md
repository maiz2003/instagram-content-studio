# Recovery Protocol (suppression, dormancy, diagnosis)

What to do when reach drops. The book's position: the algorithm doesn't punish randomly; it responds to the signals an account accumulates [U.ch7]. This file is read by `growth-recovery-doctor`.

**Citations:** `[F<part>.<n>]` · `[U.n]` · `[U.chN]`. **Everything is a `book-claim`.** Shadowban ruling: C-8.

## 1. Diagnose before treating
**Step 0: Account Status.** Check Settings → Account Status. If restrictions show, fix those first (remove the flagged content, appeal). If everything is green, the problem is content, the hook or posting behaviour, not a hidden ban [U.147], C-8.

**Step 1: find which signal fell** (compare the last ~10 posts with the account's own earlier baseline, using Insights):
| Symptom | Likely cause | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| Views drop at 1–3 s; low average watch time | Weak hook / slow opening / time-waster intros | `hook-formulas.md`; [U.51], [F1.26], [U.155] |
| Good watch time, few sends/saves | No reason to send or keep | `ranking-signals.md` §3 |
| Reach only from followers, near-zero non-followers | Fails the "reach non-followers" conditions (watermarks, reposts, off-app links, low quality) | [U.5], [U.13], [F1.30] |
| Sudden drop after a topic change | Niche drift; users removing the topic | [F2.13], [U.4], [U.54] |
| Gradual decline, many posts | Ghost posting, audience fatigue, repetitive format | [U.ch7], [U.122] |
| Drop after one post | Reports or "Not interested" taps | [F2.21], [F1.25] |
| Many new followers, then falling engagement | Off-brand followers who don't engage | [U.158] |
| Explore disappeared | Engagement bait detected | [U.ch7] |
| Engagement rate < ~4% sustained | Loses "continuous organic" status (book's figure) | [F3.6], [F3.36] |
| Posting-and-leaving pattern | Seen as uninterested in your own content; also "socially inactive" | [F2.25], [F2.5] |

## 2. Causes of suppression [U.ch7]
| Cause | Behaviour | Claimed effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Ghost posting** | Posting often (e.g. 3×/day) with no real engagement | Up to −40% reach |
| **Engagement baiting** | "Tag someone who…" style lines | Hidden from Explore |
| **Aggregator penalty** | Reposting others' content (10+ recycled videos a day) | Excluded from recommendations for 30 days |
| Reports | Even unjustified reports can flag the account temporarily; two within 48 h reduce visibility for a week (low) | [F2.21] |
| Watermarks | Other apps' logos reduce distribution | [U.37], [F1.6] |
| Posting without intent | Every weak post adds to a "weak reputation" | [F3.15] |
| Artificial engagement | Pods and bought followers are detected and penalised | [U.100], [U.138] |

## 3. The recovery protocol ("algorithmic factory reset") [U.ch7]
1. **Stop regular posting for 48 hours** to halt the flow of negative signals [U.ch7]. The book says a pause after weak posts is read as a chance to reassess, not as death [F3.15].
2. **Use "Reset suggested content"** so the account's own interest history is clean [U.10], [U.ch7].
3. **Rebuild quality with Trial Reels:** post high-quality work to non-followers only, building a new quality signal from an audience with no prior impression [U.3], [U.ch7].
4. **Fix the cause from §1** before posting publicly again: the hook, the niche, watermarks, bait, off-brand content.
5. **Return with smart consistency:** fewer, stronger posts (a few per week at high quality), at most one Reel a day (C-4) [U.126], [U.110], [U.conclusion.3].
6. **Do the daily relationship work:** 10 minutes of pre-post activity, 60 minutes of post-post presence, Story stickers every day, replies [U.124], [F2.25], [F2.5], `stories-playbook.md`.
7. **Recycle past winners:** repost a strong Reel from 45+ days ago with a new opening [F1.40]. Repeat winning patterns with variation [U.43].
8. **Archive weak or off-brand posts** that no longer represent the account [F4.8].
9. **Optional clean start:** the book says new accounts get an "algorithmically clean" test boost [F3.16]. Only consider this for an unrecoverable account, and weigh it against losing the audience.

## 4. Signs recovery is working [U.ch7]
| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Rising full-watch rate | Quality restored |
| New followers increasing | Recommendations returning |
| Reach from non-followers | Suppression phase over |
| Appearing in Explore | Algorithmic trust restored |

## 5. Never as a "fix"
Buying engagement, pods, secondary-account engagement, mass follow/unfollow, reposting others' viral content, or deleting and re-uploading the same post repeatedly. See E-1, E-6, E-8, and [F3.16] (deleting and re-editing hurts new accounts).
