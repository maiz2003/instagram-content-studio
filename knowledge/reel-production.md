## 1. Structure
- Hook in 0–3 s → promise → **re-hook ~8 s** → beats with a small reward every few seconds → payoff → ending (loop / cut-off / question) → one CTA. See `hook-formulas.md` §4–5 [F2.26], [U.92], [U.106].
- **One idea per Reel** [U.97]. **Cut the filler**: every second must add value [U.111], [U.110].
- **The strong ending matches the strong opening**, closing on a clear call to act [U.114].
- **Give real depth:** tie the tip to a bigger principle so it isn't forgotten [U.112]. Offer value that makes people stop and apply it (the "stop metric") [U.52].
- Clarity over complexity: simple analogies make hard ideas instant [U.84], [U.98].
- Emotional polarity (joy, anger, shock) beats neutral delivery [U.99]. Use "we" for shared struggles [U.109].

## 2. Pacing and editing
| Cue | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pattern interrupt every 4–6 s | Change angle, add a pop sound, or change filter/text | [F2.45], [U.92], [U.106] |
| Fast cuts where the content allows | Cut every 1–3 s for energetic content, ~3 s for talking content | [F2.49], [U.71], [U.131] |
| Jarring cut around 3–4 s | Breaks the scroll rhythm | [F2.17] |
| Zoom / bounce on the key point | A sudden zoom-out or bounce as the strongest line lands | [F2.35] |
| Slow down 5–10% on key moments | Reveal, text cards, fast hand motion, transitions; add a 2–3% zoom | [F2.37] |
| Lighting or colour shift at the reveal | Dim → bright (or reverse) | [F2.44], [U.44] |
| Rising tempo | Music or pace that builds to the payoff | [F2.31] |
| Countdown / progress bar | The eye chases the end | [F2.43] |
| Big emoji / symbol pop | At the reveal; use sparingly for premium brands | [F2.40] |
| Animated arrows / framing on key text | Guide the eye in the first second (the book claims +13–22% CTR) | [F2.7] |
| Coloured frame around the video | Helps it stand out in the Feed | [U.82] |
| Clarity beats cinematic complexity | A clear phone video beats confusing edits | [U.49], [U.125] |
| But quality still signals seriousness | Good light, colour correction, stable framing | [U.141] |

| Cue | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Always a sound layer | Never fully silent; low music under the voice | [F2.48] |
| Original voiceover first | Your voice signals originality | [F1.10], [U.13] (C-11) |

## 8. Reel formats that work

|---|---|---|

| Comparisons | X vs Y drives discussion | [U.73], [H.11] |

| Memes tied to your niche | Humour for younger audiences, but only on-brand (U.158) | [U.80] |
| Sarcastic / reversed trend responses | Seen as creative, pushed forward | [F1.27] |

## 9. Series, repetition and reuse
- **Series (Part 1, 2…)** raise return visits and session time [F1.17], [U.95] (a fixed day/name builds a habit), [U.133].
- **"Part 2 in 72 hours" / "save it and come back"** targets the claimed 3-day return signal [F1.35].
- **Link Reels into a journey:** Reel → Story → DM [U.102], [U.113].
- **Reels added to Favourites** get a longer lifespan [F1.28].
- **Repost a strong Reel after ~45 days** with a small change (new opening or filter); most followers won't have seen it [F1.40].
- **Repeat winning patterns** with variation [U.43], [U.134]; watch for fatigue [U.122].
- **Mix formats across the week** (Reel / carousel / Story) [U.65].
