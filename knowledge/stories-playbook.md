# Stories Playbook

Stories are the relationship channel: every interaction tells the system the follower cares, which lifts your next posts in their Feed. This file is read by `story-sequencer`.

**Citations:** `[F<part>.<n>]` · `[U.n]` · `[U.chN]` · `[H.n]`. **Everything is a `book-claim`.**

## 1. Why Stories matter
- Stories are the main way the system measures relationship strength. Every vote, reply or DM from a Story raises the odds that follower sees your future posts [U.ch6], [F3.31], [F3.33].
- Accounts with repeated Story engagement get more Feed reach than accounts that post only Reels [U.ch6].
- Morning Story engagement lifts same-day Reel performance [F3.14]. A Story warm-up (poll or question) before a Reel counts as "warm" activity [F1.9].
- Small accounts (under 10k) saw Story reach rise ~35% in 2025 (book's figure) [U.11].
- Link-sticker click-through keeps supporting your next Stories; swipe/link taps are strong signals [F3.10], [F3.28].

## 2. Rules of thumb
| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Post Stories daily | [U.11], [U.ch6] |
| 6–13 frames per day is the ideal range | [U.11] |
| At least one interactive sticker every day (poll / slider / question / quiz) | [U.ch6], [F3.23], [F3.33] |
| **The 4-linked-Stories rule:** curiosity → go deeper into the problem → solution → CTA | [U.ch6] |
| Reply to every Story reply: DMs from Stories are the strongest "closeness" signal; accounts that get many become followers' "inner circle" | [U.ch6] |
| Share every new Reel to Stories right after posting, with the bottom bar visible, then again ~12 h later from a new angle | [F1.11], [F2.25], [F2.14] |
| End Reels by pointing to a Story or event on the account; move people between the two | [F3.24] |
| Daily-life / behind-the-scenes frames make followers feel part of your life | [U.64], [F3.20] |
| Keep text short and clear; enable auto-translation where available | [F3.18] |
| Stickers and effects keep Stories native; countdowns and progress bars work in Stories too | [F2.43] |
| Allow resharing of your posts to Stories | [F2.8] |

## 3. Sticker toolkit [U.ch6]
| Element | Best use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Poll | A quick either/or (a "micro-decision" [U.42]) | "Which do you prefer: this or that?" [F3.23] |
| Emoji slider | Measure a feeling or interest | "How hard was today? 😩→😎" |
| Question box | Collect audience questions and needs | "Ask me anything, write it here" |
| Quiz sticker | Test knowledge; drive taps | "Which one is right?" |
| DM trigger | Ask for a word by DM | "Send the word 'guide' and I'll send it" [H.18], [H.22] |
| Link sticker | Drive to the offer/quiz; the click rate is watched | [F3.10], [F3.28] |
| Countdown | A launch or event (real dates only, E-4) | [U.70] |

## 4. Close friends, Plus and early access
- Build a close-friends list of the most engaged followers. Give them previews and early access; their early engagement lifts the public post [U.12], [H.38].
- Instagram Plus (paid) adds rewatch counts, multiple audience lists, extended Stories and Spotlight pins [U.17]. Only worth it for businesses that will act on the data.

## 5. Highlights (Stories that stay)
Order, covers and the intro story are in `profile-architecture.md` §6. Refresh highlight covers and add new Stories so they re-surface to followers [U.38]. A "wait for it…" beat keeps viewers 2–3 s longer [F4.37].

## 6. The Story → DM sales path
The book's journey is Reel → Story → DM [U.102], [U.113]. Stories carry the extra detail and the ask, and the DM closes the sale privately, with the payment link inside the chat where possible [H.22], [H.29]. The Story ask is always a real keyword that delivers something real.

## 7. Daily Story sequence template (6–8 frames)
1. **Hook frame:** a curiosity line or a real moment (≤10 words).
2. **Context:** the problem, in the audience's words.
3. **Interactive:** poll / slider / quiz on that problem.
4. **Value:** one tip, a screenshot or a result.
5. **Proof / behind the scenes:** real only.
6. **Bridge:** "new Reel on this: tap" (reshared Reel) or a link sticker.
7. **CTA:** DM keyword or link sticker (one ask).
8. *(optional)* **Reply screenshot:** answer a question from yesterday's box (with permission).
