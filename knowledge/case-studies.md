# Case Studies

These are the 11 case studies from the 2026 Update, tagged so `case-study-matcher` can find the closest precedent for a brand or post. Numbers are **the book's reported figures**: `book-claim`, not independently verified. Never quote them to clients as fact, and never present them as the user's own results (E-5).

**Citations:** `[U.case:<name>]` = the Update's final chapter (table + detailed analysis); `[U.case lessons]` = its "Lessons" and "Shared tricks" sections.

## Tag vocabulary
- **Sector:** beauty · jewelry · family-comedy · sportswear · cafe · watches · fashion · fitness-apparel · lingerie-activewear
- **Buyer:** B2C (all 11 are B2C; for B2B, borrow the *mechanism*, not the sector)
- **Tactics:** `send-cta` · `authenticity` · `niche-focus` · `influencer-tiers` · `micro-influencers` · `co-design` · `ugc-repost` · `stories-launch` · `shoppable` · `carousel-education` · `seasonal` · `visual-identity` · `storytelling` · `trending-sound` · `comment-replies` · `live` · `offline-bridge` · `values-campaign` · `multi-channel`
- **Account size:** mega (>10M) · large (1–10M) · mid (100k–1M) · small (<100k)

## The cases

| Case | Sector / size | Tactics | Reported result (book's figure) | Transferable move |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Huda Beauty (Huda Kattan)** [U.case:Huda] | beauty / mega | authenticity, influencer-tiers, ugc-repost, values-campaign, stories-launch, shoppable, multi-channel | 55–57M followers; the 2026 Kayali launch reached eight-figure sales; Gulf stock sold out in 48 h | The founder on camera sharing honest experience; repost customer content; launch through Stories + Shop; a values stance that gets shared |
| **Joelle Mardinian** [U.case:Joelle] | beauty & skincare / mega | authenticity, live, offline-bridge, storytelling | 21.5M followers; skincare line +35% a year | Mix daily life with expertise; show products naturally (not as ads); live Stories; invite followers to real-world touchpoints |
| **Indriya Jewelry** [U.case:Indriya] | jewelry / small→mid | send-cta, seasonal, carousel-education, niche-focus | 0 → 375k followers and 50M views in a year; 4.5% engagement | **Every post has an explicit send line** ("send this to your bride-to-be friend"); occasion-driven content; carousels of designs and tips to save |
| **Momya** [U.case:Momya] | family comedy / mid | niche-focus, authenticity, comment-replies, trending-sound, local hashtags | 274.6k followers; ~883k views per post; 7.33% engagement | A narrow niche; everyday humour that teaches; reply to comments and Stories; local sounds and hashtags; invite followers' stories |
| **Nike** [U.case:Nike] | sportswear / mega | storytelling, visual-identity, influencer (athletes) | 1.6% engagement on a huge base | Real struggle-to-success stories; product woven into the story, not shown directly; high production |
| **Starbucks** [U.case:Starbucks] | cafe / mega | visual-identity, seasonal, storytelling | Strong brand recall (no number given) | A style guide (colours/fonts); seasonal limited drops that create anticipation; in-store customer stories |
| **Daniel Wellington** [U.case:DW] | watches / large | micro-influencers, shoppable, ugc-repost | Big sales lift via hundreds of micro-influencers | Many small influencers with discount codes to measure; clean product-hero images; shoppable posts |
| **Zara** [U.case:Zara] | fashion / mega | shoppable, carousel-education, seasonal | Online sales up via shoppable posts and Stories | Reels for the overall look → carousels for detail → in-app purchase; fast trend response |
| **Gymshark × Whitney Simmons** [U.case:Gymshark] | fitness apparel / mega creator | co-design, storytelling, pre-launch buzz | 1,300 comments; collection sold out within hours | The influencer co-designs; behind-the-scenes and personal story with the product; pre-launch teasers |
| **Under Armour × John's Style** [U.case:UnderArmour] | athletic apparel / mid creator | trending-sound, send-cta | 250k views, 3.9k likes, 1.4k shares, 1,715 saves | A short, honest clip in the creator's own style; clear product shot; trending sound; a share prompt |
| **Oysho × Li Lin** [U.case:Oysho] | lingerie / activewear / small (<5k) creator | carousel-education, comment-replies, micro-influencers | 10k views, 340 likes, 79 comments | A nano-creator carousel showing the product step by step; reply to nearly every comment |

## Lessons the book draws [U.case lessons]
1. **Authenticity drives private sharing.** Real stories and daily life get sent; sends per reach is the signal the book ranks highest.
2. **Reels + carousels complement each other.** Reels for reach and follows, carousels for depth, saves and purchases (C-5 on the numbers).
3. **Aligned influencers beat big ones.** Choose creators whose identity matches the brand; involve them in the product.
4. **Niche focus wins** (Momya, Indriya).
5. **In-app shopping shortens the path** (Zara, DW).
6. Shared tricks: authenticity · niche · design for sharing · multi-format · integrated collabs · social commerce · production quality + story · reply to your audience · seasonal waves.

## Related tactics elsewhere in the book
- **Micro-influencers (10k–50k)** have a more engaged, specialised audience than stars [U.62].
- **Collab posts** merge both audiences into each other's Explore [U.145]; **tag real partners** [U.66].
- **Mini success stories** (a real client or follower result) build trust [U.74]. **Only real ones** (E-5).

## Matching guidance (for `case-study-matcher`)
- Match on **mechanism first**, then sector, then size. A B2B wholesaler can borrow Indriya's send-CTA or DW's partner network, but not Huda's founder fame.
- **Small or new accounts:** Indriya (built from zero, send-CTA), Momya (niche + replies), Li Lin (nano carousel) are the most transferable. Mega-brand cases illustrate principles but not budgets.
- Always state the transfer honestly: what to copy, what doesn't transfer (budget, fame, existing audience), and the first concrete step for this brand.
