# Agency brief template (one page)

`content-studio` fills this in as `agency-brief.md`. Keep it to **one page, about 350–450 words**. The agency should be able to plan the shoot from this page alone. Write it in English, with lead-language lines quoted exactly and followed by an English gloss, unless the agency works in the lead language.

```markdown
# Agency brief: <brand> · <post slug>
**For:** <agency.name> · **From:** <brand> · **Approver:** <agency.approver> · **Publish:** <date>

**Goal:** <goal> — <one sentence on what success looks like>
**Viewer:** <target viewer + their pain, one line>
**Format / length:** Reel 9:16, <target length>

**Hook (0–3 s):** "<spoken line>" [gloss] · On-screen: "<text>" · First frame: <visual>

**Shot list (short version)**
| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| … | … | … |
(Full shot list: script.md)

**Locations & props:** <from assets_available>
**On camera:** <who> · **Voiceover:** <language + dialect>, <tone>
**Music / SFX:** <direction>
**Cover:** <frame + cover text + eye-contact note>
**Delivery specs:** 1080×1920, highest-quality export, no watermark, burned-in text as in script, sound layer
**Caption & CTA:** caption.md (ready to paste) · CTA: <mechanism + keyword>
**Do not:** <banned words/claims; no "link in bio"; no invented numbers>
**Open before shoot:** <placeholders the brand must fill>
```
