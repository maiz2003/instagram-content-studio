# Production cues (Reel)

These are editing, text, sound, cover and export cues for `reel-scriptwriter`. Every item is a `book-claim` with its source.
*Phase 2 note: this gets promoted into `knowledge/reel-production.md`. Until then it lives here, next to the one skill that uses it.*

## Pacing and editing
| Cue | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pattern interrupt every 4–6 s | Change angle, add a pop sound, or change filter/text | [F2.45], [U.92], [U.106] |
| Fast cuts where the content allows | Split into short segments; a cut every 1–3 s for energetic content, ~3 s for talking content | [F2.49], [U.71], [U.131] |
| Jarring cut around 3–4 s | Breaks the scroll rhythm and forces a re-focus | [F2.17] |
| Zoom or bounce on the key point | A sudden zoom-out/bounce when delivering the strongest line | [F2.35] |
| Slow down 5–10% on key moments | Product reveal, text-heavy cards, fast hand motion, transitions; add a 2–3% zoom | [F2.37] |
| Lighting or colour shift at the reveal | Dim → bright (or reverse) when the result appears | [F2.44], [U.44] |
| Rising tempo | Music or pace that builds toward the payoff | [F2.31] |
| Countdown / progress | "1..2..3" or a filling bar keeps the eye chasing the end | [F2.43] |
| Big emoji or symbol pop | Large 😱/🔥 with a pop at the reveal (use sparingly for premium/B2B brands) | [F2.40] |
| Clarity over effects | A clear phone-shot video beats complex cinematic editing that confuses | [U.49], [U.125] |
| But quality still signals seriousness | Good light, colour-corrected, stable | [U.141], [U.ch1 tactic] |
| Authentic imperfection | Keep natural small stumbles; over-polished content loses trust | [U.87], [U.119], [F4.27] |

## People on camera
| Cue | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Human presence | Front camera or a face for at least a few seconds | [F2.18], [U.139] |
| Expressions and gestures | Raised brows at tension; count on fingers; point at the detail | [F2.33], [F2.52], [U.68], [U.137] |
| Emotion matches message | Serious face for warnings, energy for wins | [U.108] |
| Recurring character/signature | A repeated opener line, gesture or person builds recognition | [F2.10] |
| Background signals the niche | The setting and props tell the system (and the viewer) who this is for | [U.154], [U.conclusion.2] |

## On-screen text
| Cue | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Text on throughout | Many viewers watch muted; captions/text raise completion | [F1.19], [F2.12], [F3.21] |
| 3–5 keywords, changing every few seconds | Clear contrasting font; don't cover faces or the product | [F2.28] |
| Minimal words per card | 1–2 headlines, not paragraphs | [U.72] |
| Typography hierarchy | Keyword bold and large, detail smaller | [U.81] |
| Framing/arrows on key text | A box or arrow around the key text in the first second | [F2.7] |
| Text is read by the system | On-screen text feeds categorisation (OCR) | [F3.35], [U.ch3] |

## Sound
| Cue | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Always a sound layer | Never fully silent; low music under the voiceover | [F2.48] |
| Original voiceover first | Your own voice or voiceover signals originality | [F1.10], [U.13] (C-11) |
| SFX on transitions/reveals | Ding, whoosh or pop at key moments (Meta library preferred) | [F2.23], [U.78] |
| Mood-matched music | Energetic for motivation; don't fight the message | [U.33] |
| Vary sounds | Don't reuse the same sound more than 3× | [F2.20] |
| Clear speech | Moderate pace, clear pronunciation; quiet location (also enables auto-translation) | [U.103], [U.9] |

## Cover
| Cue | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Designed cover | A clear, intriguing line of text; a face if possible | [F1.16], [F3.7] |
| Direct eye contact | A separate cover shot looking at the lens | [F2.47] |
| Grid-ready | Face + prominent keyword + one dominant warm colour | [F4.17] |
| Question or promise on the cover | "Did you know…?" style | [U.14] |

## Specs and export
| Cue | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1080×1920 (9:16) | Never another ratio for Reels | [F2.9] |
| Highest quality export | High bitrate; don't downscale | [U.150] |
| No watermark | Clean export from CapCut etc., or finish in Edits | [U.37], [F1.6], [U.5] (E-8) |
| Edits / in-app finishing | Optional: retention graph, teleprompter; don't promise a boost | [U.15], [U.16], [U.29] (C-6) |
| Original sound or Instagram library music | Licensed tracks from the in-app library | [U.5], [F3.27] |
| Keep under 3 min for non-follower reach | Longer Reels aren't recommended to non-followers | [U.ch2] |
| Trial Reels for risky angles | Test with non-followers for 24 h first | [U.3] |
| Collab for reach | Co-post with a complementary account when relevant | [U.145] |
