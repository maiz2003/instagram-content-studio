# LOCK IN week-2 footage (AI-generated, faceless POV)

These clips back the four "Which door is yours?" Reels (09, 11, 13, 14). They were generated on 2026-09-28 at the owner's request, instead of being filmed. Everything lives in the owner's Higgsfield project "LOCK IN week 2 footage".

**How they were made:**
1. **Stills.** Nano Banana Pro generated the starting photos. The Bored desk photo (job `367aa497…`) is the master reference. Every other still was generated with it as an image reference, so the hands, hoodie, desk, lamp, spiral notebook and phone in its matte black case stay the same across shots.
2. **Motion.** Kling 3.0 animated each still from its start frame, with one simple action per shot: 5 s, 4K for Bored and 1080p pro for the rest.
3. **Review.** Every clip was checked frame by frame. Since 2026-09-30 the review also checks the **left and right edges** as strips (every 0.1–0.25 s), because the first review missed a head at the edge of `05_lockin_night`. Clips with morphing hands, a phone that changes model, extra people or duplicated objects were rejected or trimmed out.
4. **Grade.** All clips were trimmed and given one shared grade: 30 fps, 1080×1920, slight contrast and warmth, film grain. That makes them look like they came from one phone.
