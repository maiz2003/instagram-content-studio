# LOCK IN: week-2 shot list (5 faceless clips)

**Time needed:** about 75 minutes for all five, including resets. One evening and one daytime session is enough.
**Upload to:** the shared Drive folder, in a new subfolder `clips-week2`. Name files `01_bored_t1.mov`, `01_bored_t2.mov`, and so on. Upload the camera originals, not copies sent through WhatsApp or Instagram, which get compressed.

**What happens next:** I cut these into week-2 Reels. The brand's kinetic text goes over the footage, with your sound kit on top. Same look, but now with real motion behind the words, which gives the thumb a reason to stop in the first second [U.118]. The four door clips become a **four-part "Which door is yours?" series**, which points straight at the quiz.

## Rules for every clip
| | Do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | **Vertical 9:16**, normal camera app, 4K or 1080p at 30 fps, **no filters**, not the Instagram camera | Native full-screen quality; original footage [F2.9], [U.5] |
| HDR off | iPhone: Settings → Camera → Record Video → **HDR Video off**. Android: turn off HDR10+/HDR video | HDR clips look washed out once posted |
| Hold it still | Phone on a tripod or a stack of books. Tap and hold to **lock focus and exposure** | Stable framing signals quality [U.141] |
| Handles | **3 seconds of stillness before and after** each action, and **3 takes** of each clip | Room to cut on the beat |
| Surface | Dark, plain desk (black, walnut, dark grey). Clear everything else off | Matches the brand's near-black #111216 |
| Light | One warm lamp from the side, or a window from the side. Night clips: screen light only | Warm light echoes the amber; a light change marks the reveal [F2.44] |
| People | **Hands and forearms only.** No face, no mirror, no reflections in the screen | Faceless brand; hands still add human presence [F2.18] |
| Screens | Use Do Not Disturb or a clean lock screen. **No real names or messages visible** | Privacy |
| Sound | Record natural sound (the phone buzz, the drawer, the door, the pen). **No music** | Your kit and real sounds go on top in the edit |
| Leave out | Other brands' logos, TV or music playing in the background | Avoids copyright muting |

## The clips

### 01 · BORED: "the reach" (10–15 s)
- **Frame:** overhead, straight down on the desk. An open notebook or laptop in the middle, the phone face down at the right edge.
- **Action:** Hand writes for 2 s, stops, and taps the pen twice. The other hand drifts toward the phone and **stops a few centimetres away** (hold 1 s). It pulls back, starts a timer on a watch or kitchen timer, and writes again.
- **Used for:** the **Bored** door Reel ("Not lazy. Bored for three minutes.") and the 3 Ugly Minutes carousel teaser. Text lands on the hand stopping.

### 02 · STUCK: "the blank page" (10–15 s)
- **Frame:** close-up from behind the shoulder, laptop screen filling most of the frame (screen-record instead if easier).
- **Action:** The cursor blinks on an empty document for 3 s. Then a burst of tab-switching (Cmd/Ctrl+Tab through 6–10 tabs), and back to the blank page. The hand closes the laptop halfway.
- **Also record:** a **screen recording** of the same thing (built-in screen recorder, no notifications).
- **Used for:** the **Stuck** door Reel ("You didn't get distracted. You got stuck."). The tab burst is the pattern interrupt [F2.45].

### 03 · ANXIOUS: "the check" (10–15 s)
- **Frame:** side-on at desk height, phone face down in the foreground, slightly out of focus. Room fairly dim.
- **Action:** The phone **buzzes and its edge glows** (send yourself a message; record the buzz). The hand flips it and scrolls with the thumb for 3 s (**only a lock screen or a blank app, nothing private**), then puts it back face down. Two seconds later the hand reaches for it again: cut.
- **Used for:** the **Anxious** door Reel ("Checking isn't relief. It's the loop.").

### 04 · TIRED: "23:40" (15–20 s, at night)
- **Frame 1:** in bed or on the sofa, lights off. The phone screen is the only light. Film the hand holding the phone, with the **lock-screen clock showing about 23:40**.
- **Frame 2:** hallway: the hand places the phone on a shelf or charger **outside the bedroom**, walks back, and **the door closes** (record the click).
- **Used for:** the **Tired** door Reel ("Not tired. Doom scrolling.") and a remake of Day-7's "you're not tired". Screen-lit to dark is the reveal.

### 05 · THE LOCK-IN: brand signature (5–8 s)
- **Frame:** overhead or 45°, same desk as clip 01.
- **Action:** The hand puts the phone **into a drawer or box and closes it firmly** (record the clunk; it matches the lock-click sound). The same hand starts the timer, and both hands start working. Film it in **daylight and at night** (2 versions).
- **Used for:** the **opening or closing shot of every week-2 Reel**. A recurring visual signature builds recognition [F2.10], [U.54], and the loop ends where it began [U.121].
