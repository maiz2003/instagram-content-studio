At about 3–4 clips per Reel, that's an estimate of 1–2 more weeks of footage Reels before it repeats. Re-count with `python3 scripts/footage_usage.py` before planning each week.

**How to film:** the rules table in `outputs/lock-in/2026-10-week2-shot-list.md` still applies:
- vertical 9:16, HDR off, no filters
- phone on a tripod or a stack of books, focus and exposure locked
- 3 seconds of stillness before and after each action, 3 takes
- dark plain desk, one warm side lamp
- **hands and forearms only**, no faces, no readable screens or names, no logos
- natural sound, no music

**Matching the AI look:** dark hoodie sleeves, a dark desk, a cream lamp, a white spiral notebook, a black phone in a plain matte case. The viewer should not notice the switch.
