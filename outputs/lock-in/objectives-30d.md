# LOCK IN: 30-day objectives (launch month)

Set with the owner on 2026-09-27.
- **Goal:** audience first. Sales are tracked but aren't the target yet.
- **Budget:** organic only.
- **Owner time:** 2+ hours a day.
- **Market:** Europe + US, posting at 18:05 UK / 19:05 CET / 13:05 ET.

Day 1 is the day the Four Doors Reel goes live. Weeks count from there.

The tracker page holds the numbers. Log each post about 48 hours after it goes live, copying the figures from Instagram Insights.

All thresholds marked "book" are the book's figures (`book-claim`), not platform-confirmed. The other targets are our own, set against **your week-1 median**. A brand-new account has no history, so week 1 is the baseline.

## What we optimise for
Reach new people who watch to the end, then send or save the post.

Watch time, sends and saves carry most of the ranking weight in the book's table: 35 / 20 / 15 [U.ch1]. Followers come from that, and sales follow followers. Every objective below serves that chain.

## Objectives

| # | Objective | Measured by (Insights) | Month-1 target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Show up every day** | Days with a feed post; Story days | 28 of 30 days, at most 1 Reel/day (C-4); Stories daily [U.11] |
| 2 | **Stop the scroll** | Skip rate per Reel | Week-4 median at or below 40% (book: 60% held past 3 s doubles spread [U.20]) |
| 3 | **Hold to the end** | Average watch time ÷ length | Week-4 median above week 1 |
| 4 | **Get sent and saved** | Shares (sends) and saves per 1,000 views | Week-4 median ≥ 1.5× week 1 |
| 5 | **Reach new people** | Views per Reel; % from non-followers | Median views rising week on week; non-followers the majority of views |
| 6 | **Turn viewers into followers** | Follows per Reel; followers each Sunday | Rising week on week |
| 7 | **Keep the funnel working** | LOCK comments, DMs answered, quiz taps, Whop sales | Every LOCK comment answered within the hour; conversion tracked from week 1 |
| 8 | **Learn what works** | Trial Reels run; formats ranked | 2 Trial Reels a week [U.3]; top 2 formats named by the end of week 3, then doubled [U.43] |

Engagement rate is shown for reference only: (likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ views. The book says a steady 4–6% keeps an account in continuous distribution [F3.6]. It is not a target.

## Weekly rhythm (your ~2 h a day)
- **Before each post:** 10 minutes of genuine replies in the niche [U.124].
- **After posting:** stay 60 minutes and answer every comment [F2.25]. Send LOCK DMs within the hour.
- **Stories every day:** the day plans are in `2026-09-27-launch-week/week-plan.md`.
- **Filming:** shoot 5 faceless clips a week from the shot list I send. I cut them into the next week's Reels.
- **Sunday:** fill in the tracker's weekly row (followers, profile visits, link taps). I read the tracker, add the Whop sales myself (read-only), and write the next week's plan.
