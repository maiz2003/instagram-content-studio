## 1. Indriya Jewelry
- **What they did:** every post carried an explicit "send this to…" line, tied to occasions, and carousels of designs and tips were built to be saved. The book reports 0 → 375k followers and 50M views in a year.
- **Your move:** make **a specific send line a fixed element** of every post ("send this to the friend who's always 'so behind'"). Build one saveable carousel a week from the book's 13 diagrams.
- **What doesn't transfer:** jewelry's gifting occasions. Your equivalents are **exam seasons, back-to-school, New Year, Monday resets**.
- **First step:** in `week-plan.md` every caption already has a send line. Next, turn "The 3 Ugly Minutes" into a carousel with carousel-builder.

## 2. Momya
- **What they did:** a narrow niche (mothers), everyday situations played for humour that also teaches, and replies to comments and Stories. The book reports a 7.33% engagement rate.
- **Your move:** your quiet call-outs (clips 11–18) are the same mechanism: precise, slightly painful everyday truths. Keep the niche narrow (16–26, studying/building, doom-scrolling). Answer every comment with a follow-up question.
- **What doesn't transfer:** a face and family life. Your recurring "character" is the look plus the voice [F2.10].
- **First step:** a weekly "best comment" Story with permission [U.79].

## 3. Oysho × Li Lin
- **What they did:** a creator with under 5k followers made a step-by-step carousel and replied to nearly every comment. Reported: 10k views, 79 comments.
- **Your move:** small accounts can win on carousels + replies. Later, send the book to 5–10 small study/productivity creators (10k–50k) for honest takes [U.62]. Only real reviews; no scripted testimonials (E-5).
- **First step:** after launch week, list 10 micro-creators in study/discipline and prepare the offer (a free copy, no obligation to post).

**Recommendation:** run launch week as planned. Then add **one diagram carousel a week** (Indriya), **reply to everything** (Momya, Li Lin), and try **micro-creator seeding** once there are real reviews to earn.
