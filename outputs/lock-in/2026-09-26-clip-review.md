## What's strong
- **The copy.** Most headlines already follow the hook formulas. #05, #06, #08, #22 and the call-outs (11–18) are exactly the "precise diagnosis" the book rates highest [H.1]. #22 is almost word for word the hook we scored 11/12.
- **Brand consistency** and faceless-friendliness. No filming needed.
- **Text sits in the upper-middle**, clear of Instagram's bottom UI.

## What will cost reach (in priority order)

1. **Nothing moves.** In all 28, frame 1 and the last frame are identical except for a very slow zoom. Instagram's test audience decides in about 0.5–3 s [U.118], [U.ch2], and a static card gives the thumb no reason to stop [F1.13]. The whole line is visible at once, so there's no open loop either [U.45].
   **Fix:** build the text in. Set-up line first (0–1.5 s), punchline in amber at ~2 s (e.g. "YOU ARE NOT LAZY." → "YOU ARE IN WITHDRAWAL."). Add a 3% punch-in when the amber word lands [F2.35], plus one SFX hit [F2.23].
2. **Too long for a static card.** 12–16 s of the same card means most people leave long before the end, and completion falls [C-1], [U.144].
   **Fix:** statement and opinion cards at **6–8 s** (they'll loop and earn replays [F1.21]); call-outs at 7 s are fine. Keep 15–20 s only for cards with a sequence of beats (see #4).
3. **Clips 11–18 are silent.** The book says not to post fully silent Reels [F2.48].
   **Fix:** add the same low music bed and a soft "tick" on each line, or record a flat, calm voiceover (original audio [F1.10]). Add music from Instagram's own library when posting, to avoid copyright muting.
4. **The how-it-works diagrams are too small.** The diagram card fills about ⅓ of the width, and its text is roughly 25 px tall on a 1080-wide frame, which is unreadable on a phone. It's also static for ~20 s.
   **Fix:** go full-screen, one beat per card (e.g. Four Doors = title card → one card per door → the fix → CTA). The type-only version of the Four Doors Reel in `2026-09-26-phone-not-discipline/script.md` shows how.
5. **No call to action anywhere.** No "Comment LOCK", no send or save prompt, so the funnel never starts.
   **Fix:** a 1.5 s end card on every Reel: "Comment LOCK → 4-question quiz". Rotate in a send line on the call-outs ("send this to whoever's still 'starting Monday'") and a save line on the how-it-works clips. Keep it to two asks at most.
6. **Search keyword.** Most cards don't show "doom scroll", "discipline" or "study" on screen. Let the caption's first 5 words carry it [U.ch3], and when a card already contains the word (07, 22), keep it.

## Posting plan for a new account
- **At most one Reel a day.** Skip a day rather than post a weak one [C-4]. Don't delete or re-edit after posting [F3.16].
- **Rotate formats** so the feed doesn't feel repetitive [U.122]: how-it-works (saves) → statement (reach) → call-out (sends) → opinion (comments).
