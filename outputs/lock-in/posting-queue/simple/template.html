Something not working?

Save video did nothing

iPhone: after you tap Save, a menu opens. Tap Save Video. If no menu opens, tap the video above to play it, then press and hold on it and choose Save.

Android: the video goes to your Downloads (Files app). It also shows in your Gallery after a moment.

Computer: it goes to your Downloads folder. Use Instagram on your phone to post, so send the file to your phone (AirDrop, Google Drive or email).

Copy caption did nothing

Tap Show the caption under the step, press and hold the text, choose Select all, then Copy.

I can't find the video in Instagram

In Instagram tap + → Reel. The gallery opens with your newest video first. If it isn't there, open your phone's Photos or Files app and check Downloads.

The caption looks cut off or has odd gaps

Paste again. Make sure you copied the whole caption: it should end with the hashtags. Line breaks are meant to be there.

I forgot the AI label or Trial switch

Don't delete the post and don't edit it. Message the owner right away and say which post it was.

I missed the 18:05 time

Post as soon as you can the same day, and tell the owner the time you posted. Never post two Reels in one day.

The tracker won't open

It needs the owner's invite. Until you have it, message the owner the numbers (views, shares, saves, LOCK comments, follows) and they will enter them.

Anything else

Don't guess. Message the owner. Never buy followers, use a second account on our posts, or write "link in bio" or "buy now".
